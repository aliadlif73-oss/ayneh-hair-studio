# Cinematic salon stills

Five dedicated generated film stills for section two, stored as `design/landing/assets/salon-film-01.webp` through `05.webp` and mirrored to `public/landing` by the landing builder. These depict a fictional salon and fictional recurring characters; they are not customer photos. Originals are retained in the generation workspace. Delivery uses optimized WebP copies without artistic edits.

Visual continuity: same customer and stylist, cream plaster, espresso wood, brass arches, golden light, 35mm editorial photography. Each composition reserves the right side for live accessible Persian text. No text is baked into photographs.

## Shot 1

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: first widescreen film still in a five-shot cinematic sequence for AYNEH, a Persian beauty-salon visual consultation product. Generate one photorealistic 16:9 1920x1080 cinematic photograph, NOT a webpage or collage. A premium intimate beauty salon in warm ivory plaster, dark espresso wood, brushed brass arched mirrors, travertine counter. Left half: a Persian/Middle Eastern woman customer about 30, warm olive skin, long chestnut wavy hair, wearing a simple ivory blouse, seated in a salon chair. Beside her stands a female stylist about 35 with a neat dark brunette bob and espresso clothing. The customer shows the stylist a smartphone with a small hairstyle photo; she looks thoughtfully uncertain while the stylist listens. Their faces are natural, restrained documentary emotion. Keep the people in the left 55% of the wide composition. The rightmost 40% is a quiet darker espresso wall with a softly lit brass arch edge and generous negative space for later HTML copy, NO printed text. Golden late-afternoon light through blinds from camera-left, gentle haze, rich espresso shadows, restrained honey/brass highlights, film grain, anamorphic 35mm film still, subtle lens falloff, premium editorial beauty cinematography. Palette strictly cream, espresso, brushed brass. No purple, no blue tech effects, no neon, no floating UI, no typography, no logos, no watermarks, no graphic frames. Establish a distinctive real salon and two recurring characters for later matching shots.
